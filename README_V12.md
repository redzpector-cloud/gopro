# JEJAK CAM PRO V12

## Horizon Lock + Gyro Stabilization

- Horizon ON/OFF control for the live camera preview.
- Rotation Vector sensor measures device roll.
- Preview is counter-rotated with a small overscan to keep the live view level.
- CameraX/Camera2 video stabilization remains enabled when supported by the device.
- Continuous video autofocus remains enabled.

### Important
The V12 horizon lock is a live-preview leveling feature. It does not rewrite recorded video frames. The saved video relies on the phone camera's EIS/OIS/video stabilization pipeline.
